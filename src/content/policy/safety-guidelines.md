
# BUYKARLO SAFETY GUIDELINES FOR OFFLINE TRANSACTIONS

**Effective Date:**  1 June 2026

## 1. PURPOSE

BuyKarlo is a platform that enables users to discover products and connect with one another. Transactions, inspections, exchanges, deliveries, and payments are generally conducted directly between users.
These Safety Guidelines are intended to promote responsible conduct and help users reduce risks associated with buying, selling, and meeting other individuals.
While BuyKarlo may provide tools that facilitate communication, BuyKarlo does not supervise, monitor, or control offline interactions between users.
Users remain solely responsible for their decisions and actions.

## 2. GENERAL SAFETY PRINCIPLES

Before entering into any transaction, users should:
* Exercise caution and common sense;
* Independently verify information provided by other users;
* Carefully inspect products;
* Verify the identity of the other party where appropriate;
* Avoid rushing into transactions;
* Trust their instincts if something appears suspicious.
If a transaction appears unsafe, users should discontinue communication immediately.

## 3. MEET IN SAFE PUBLIC LOCATIONS

Whenever possible, users should meet:
* In public places;
* During daylight hours;
* In locations with regular public activity;
* In areas with security personnel or surveillance cameras.
Examples include:
* University campuses;
* College common areas;
* Shopping centres;
* Cafes;
* Public libraries;
* Police-designated safe exchange zones where available.
Users should avoid:
* Isolated locations;
* Private residences of unknown individuals;
* Remote areas;
* Locations that create personal safety concerns.

## 4. VERIFY THE PRODUCT BEFORE PAYMENT

Before making payment, buyers are strongly encouraged to:
* Inspect the product carefully;
* Verify its condition;
* Test functionality where applicable;
* Confirm included accessories;
* Verify model numbers and serial numbers;
* Check for signs of tampering or damage.
For electronics, users should perform practical functionality checks whenever reasonably possible.

## 5. VERIFY OWNERSHIP WHERE APPROPRIATE

For high-value products, users should consider requesting reasonable evidence of ownership.
Examples may include:
* Purchase invoices;
* Original receipts;
* Product packaging;
* Warranty documentation;
* Educational institution ownership clearance where applicable.
The absence of proof does not necessarily indicate wrongdoing; however, users should exercise heightened caution when ownership cannot be reasonably established.

## 6. BE CAUTIOUS WITH ADVANCE PAYMENTS

Users should carefully evaluate requests for advance payments.
BuyKarlo recommends avoiding advance payments unless the user is fully satisfied regarding:
* The legitimacy of the transaction;
* The identity of the counterparty;
* The risks involved.
Users should be particularly cautious of:
* Urgent payment requests;
* Pressure tactics;
* Requests involving gift cards;
* Requests involving cryptocurrency transfers;
* Unusual payment arrangements.

## 7. PROTECT YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

Users should not disclose unnecessary personal information.
Users should avoid sharing:
* Passwords;
* One-time passwords (OTPs);
* Banking credentials;
* UPI PINs;
* Credit card information;
* Government identification numbers unless legally necessary.
BuyKarlo will never request passwords, OTPs, or banking credentials through user communications.

## 8. BRING A FRIEND WHEN APPROPRIATE

For higher-value transactions or meetings with unfamiliar individuals, users should consider:
* Bringing a trusted friend;
* Informing family members or friends of the meeting location;
* Sharing expected meeting times;
* Maintaining communication with trusted contacts.
Personal safety should always take priority over completing a transaction.

## 9. USE CAUTION WITH HIGH-VALUE ITEMS

Transactions involving expensive products such as:
* Laptops;
* Smartphones;
* Cameras;
* Gaming devices;
* Vehicles;
* Professional equipment;
may require additional precautions.
Users should consider:
* Verifying device identifiers;
* Checking functionality;
* Meeting in secure locations;
* Requesting supporting documentation.

## 10. ONLINE SAFETY

Users should remain vigilant against:
* Phishing attempts;
* Identity theft;
* Fake payment confirmations;
* Fraudulent screenshots;
* Requests for sensitive credentials.
Users should independently verify payment status through official banking channels rather than relying solely on screenshots or messages.

## 11. REPORT SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY

Users should promptly report:
* Fraudulent listings;
* Suspected scams;
* Harassment;
* Threatening behaviour;
* Counterfeit products;
* Stolen property;
* Violations of Platform policies.
Timely reporting helps protect the broader BuyKarlo community.

## 12. EMERGENCIES AND CRIMINAL MATTERS

Where users believe criminal activity has occurred or immediate danger exists, they should contact:
* Local law enforcement authorities;
* Emergency services;
* Appropriate government authorities.
BuyKarlo is not an emergency response service and cannot provide immediate intervention in dangerous situations.

## 13. BUYKARLO’S ROLE

BuyKarlo provides technology services that enable users to discover products and communicate with one another.
BuyKarlo:
* Does not supervise meetings;
* Does not verify all listings;
* Does not guarantee user conduct;
* Does not guarantee product authenticity;
* Does not guarantee transaction outcomes;
* Does not guarantee payment or delivery.
Users assume responsibility for evaluating and managing risks associated with their interactions.

## 14. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

BuyKarlo may revise these Safety Guidelines periodically to address emerging risks, improve user protection, and reflect platform developments.
Users are encouraged to review these Guidelines regularly and prioritize safe and responsible conduct at all times.
